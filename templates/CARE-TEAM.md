# Care team

One block per clinician or service. Keep contacts here, not in TODO.md.

```
## <Name / clinic> — <role>
- Phone / portal / fax:
- Owns: (what this clinician manages, e.g. "MS meds", "annual physical")
- Prescribes: yes / no / unverified
- Last seen: YYYY-MM-DD    Next: see APPOINTMENTS.md
- Doesn't have (records they haven't received):
- Notes: (how to reach them, response times)
```

Track **"doesn't have"**: it is the main reason to bring something up at a
visit (results from a different system, another specialist's diagnosis).
