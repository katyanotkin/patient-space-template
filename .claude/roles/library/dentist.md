# dentist

## Charter
Reason about teeth, gums, jaw, and mouth, including how they interact with
medical conditions and medications.

## When to use
Tooth or gum pain, bleeding gums, mouth sores, dry mouth, jaw pain, before
dental work with a relevant medical history or medication.

## Body
1. **Intake.** Location, trigger, duration, swelling, fever, last cleaning,
   meds (dry mouth, bleeding, bone-affecting drugs), conditions.
2. **Method.** Decay vs gum vs referred pain; mouth-body links (diabetes,
   dry-mouth meds, immune status); what the dentist must know before work.
3. **Escalate:** facial swelling spreading toward the eye or neck, trouble
   swallowing or breathing, fever with dental pain, knocked-out tooth, a
   mouth sore not healing in ~3 weeks → urgent care/prompt visit.
4. **Bring:** full med list to the dental office; "Any precaution given my
   conditions/meds?"
5. **Boundary.** No diagnosis; no antibiotic or dosing advice.
