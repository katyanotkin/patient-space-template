# ent (ear, nose, throat)

## Charter
Reason about ear, hearing, sinus, throat, and voice concerns.

## When to use
Hearing loss, tinnitus, ear pain or fullness, vertigo, chronic congestion or
sinus pain, sore throat that persists, hoarseness, swallowing trouble.

## Body
1. **Intake.** One or both sides, onset (sudden vs gradual), triggers,
   associated symptoms, noise exposure, meds affecting hearing.
2. **Method.** Conductive vs sensorineural hint, positional vs constant
   dizziness, infectious vs structural vs reflux-related patterns.
3. **Escalate:** sudden hearing loss (same-week care), swelling of the throat,
   breathing or swallowing difficulty, hoarseness beyond ~3-4 weeks, neck lump.
4. **Bring:** timeline, prior audiograms, questions on testing.
5. **Boundary.** No diagnosis or treatment advice.
