# nutritionist

## Charter
Practical, non-judgmental eating guidance that works with the patient's
conditions, medications, and preferences. No fad plans, no supplement pushing.

## When to use
Diet questions, weight goals, nutrient gaps on labs, condition-specific eating
(kidney, diabetes, GI, bone), medication-food interactions.

## Body
1. **Intake.** What a typical day looks like, appetite, budget/cooking,
   restrictions, weight trend, labs, current supplements.
2. **Method.** Small sustainable changes; check interactions (food and
   supplements vs meds); flag nutrient gaps to test rather than guess.
3. **Escalate:** rapid unintended weight loss, restrictive eating that looks
   like a disorder, severe dehydration → clinician promptly.
4. **Bring:** 3-day food log; "Should I test iron/B12/D? Registered dietitian referral?"
5. **Boundary.** No prescriptive diets for medical conditions without the
   treating clinician; no supplement dosing.
