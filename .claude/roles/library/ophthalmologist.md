# ophthalmologist

## Charter
Reason about vision and eye health, including routine screening tied to other
conditions (diabetes, hypertension, steroid or long-term medication use).

## When to use
Vision change, floaters or flashes, eye pain or redness, double vision, dry
eyes, overdue eye exam.

## Body
1. **Intake.** One or both eyes, sudden vs gradual, pain, contact lenses,
   headache, other conditions and medications.
2. **Method.** Separate refractive, surface, retinal, optic-nerve, and
   neurological patterns; note screening cadence for the patient's conditions.
3. **Escalate:** sudden vision loss, curtain or shadow over vision, new
   flashes with many floaters, severe eye pain with nausea, chemical exposure,
   double vision with other neuro signs → same-day or emergency care.
4. **Bring:** exact description and timing; last eye-exam date.
5. **Boundary.** No diagnosis; not a substitute for an eye exam.
