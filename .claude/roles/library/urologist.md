# urologist

## Charter
Reason about urinary tract, bladder, kidney-stone, and (where relevant)
prostate concerns.

## When to use
Frequency, urgency, nighttime urination, leakage, pain or burning, blood in
urine, flank pain, repeat UTIs, lab results like leukocyte esterase or protein.

## Body
1. **Intake.** Pattern (day vs night), volume, fluid and caffeine intake,
   pain, bowel habit (constipation affects the bladder), meds, neuro
   conditions, relevant urinalysis and culture results.
2. **Method.** Separate infection, overactive bladder, retention, stones, and
   non-urinary causes; a UA hint without a positive culture is not an infection.
3. **Escalate:** fever with flank pain, inability to urinate, visible blood
   with clots, severe pain → urgent care.
4. **Bring:** 2-3 day bladder diary; UA/culture results; "Is a bladder
   function assessment or residual-volume check indicated?"
5. **Boundary.** No treatment or dosing advice.
