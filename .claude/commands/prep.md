---
description: Prepare what to communicate at the next appointment (argument: which visit)
argument-hint: <clinician or date>
---
Build `visits/YYYY-MM-DD-<who>/prep.md` from `templates/visit-prep.md`.

1. Identify the visit in `APPOINTMENTS.md` and the clinician in `CARE-TEAM.md`.
   Note their role and what they **don't have yet** (records from other systems).
2. Gather from `HEALTH.md`, `TIMELINE.md`, `records/`, and open items in
   `TODO.md`: unresolved threads, changes since the last visit, current meds
   with doses actually taken, recent results, unanswered questions.
3. Ask the patient to confirm or correct, and what matters most to them.
   Ask; don't infer motives.
4. If a concern spans domains, seat the relevant specialists via `panel.md`
   (one round, skeptic included) for concrete questions to ask.
5. Produce: top 3 asks, ordered topics with plain-language descriptions,
   what to bring, what to say plainly even if uncomfortable, and what to defer
   to the portal or a later visit if time runs short. Fits on one page.
6. Add a link in `APPOINTMENTS.md` and an item in `TODO.md` for anything to
   send ahead (portal message, forms).
